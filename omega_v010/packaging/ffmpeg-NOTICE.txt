FFmpeg notice — bundled with Omega World (v0.1.6 and later)
=========================================================

This installer bundles ffmpeg.exe, the FFmpeg command-line tool, so the
in-game video tab can render MP4 clips out of the box without a separate
download.

Source of the binary
--------------------
Windows static build from https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
("ffmpeg-release-essentials.zip"), downloaded at build time by the
project's CI workflow. The exact FFmpeg version is the one shipped in
that essentials build at the time the installer was built.
